How to look

Track the moving block with your eyes. Do not stare at a fixed point — that shows sample-and-hold blur, not overdrive.

Look at the trailing edge (the side it came from).

Speed 6 s/sweep   space pause

What it means

Dark smear / trail behind → overdrive too low, raise it.

Bright halo or pale outline behind → overshoot, lower it.

Clean edge, no trail either way → correct, leave it.

Method

Judging 8 alone is hard. Calibrate your eye first:

Set OD to 0 → memorise the smear. Set to 20 → memorise the halo. Then your candidate value and see which it resembles.

Settled on this panel: 6. Some smear remains at 6 and does not fully clear at any setting — the trade is smear vs. overshoot, and 6 is the balance point.
